CHAPTER 01
General & Eligibility
⌄
1

The admin decision is final. Tournament admins reserve the right to take any decision — on gameplay, penalties, disputes or eligibility — as they see fit, and no further discussion will be entertained once a ruling is made.

2

Minimum Free Fire level to register is 50. Players below level 50 cannot submit a registration.

3

Registration requires: real name, Free Fire IGN, Free Fire UID, Free Fire level, a profile screenshot, WhatsApp number and a payment screenshot.

4

Registration fee is ₹12 per player. Pay via the official QR, upload the payment screenshot, then submit.

5

Every submitted registration starts as Pending. A player is not eligible for Play-Ins until the admin panel marks their registration Approved.

6

Only approved players appear on the Registered Players page and automatically become available for the Play-Ins.

🎮
CHAPTER 02
Play-Ins
⌄
1

Play-Ins run for 3 days, with 3 BR matches per day — 9 matches total — open to all 40 approved players.

2

Room ID and Room Password for every match are published on the Play-Ins page, alongside the match number, day and scheduled time.

3

Play-In scoring: 1 kill = 1 point, Booyah = +3 bonus points. Match points = kills + Booyah bonus.

4

Example: 7 kills + a Booyah = 10 points for that match.

5

A player's total Play-In points accumulate across all 9 matches. This total determines their minimum/base bidding price for the auction.

💰
CHAPTER 03
Auction
⌄
1

Every franchise gets a ₹100 crore purse, controlled by that team's captain.

2

Minimum bid per player is ₹2 crore. Bids can only be placed in even-numbered crore values — ₹2, ₹4, ₹6, ₹8 Cr and so on. Odd-numbered bids (₹3 Cr, ₹5 Cr, ₹7 Cr) are not allowed.

3

Each franchise must acquire exactly 5 players — no more, no less.

4

A team cannot spend beyond its ₹100 Cr purse, cannot buy more than 5 players, and cannot place an invalid odd-numbered bid.

5

The purchased player receives 50% of the final auction bid as payout. Example: a player bought for ₹50 Cr receives a ₹25 payout.

6

The moment a player is purchased, they're automatically assigned to that franchise — reflected on the Teams page, the player database and the leaderboard.

7

Franchise ownership itself can be purchased separately from the player auction — minimum ₹100, no maximum.

🗺️
CHAPTER 04
Main Season Format
⌄
1

Once the auction ends and all 8 franchises are locked, Season 12 begins.

2

Each of the 8 franchises plays 14 matches — 56 matches across the season.

3

Match results feed directly into the Points Table, Schedule page and every team's live statistics automatically.

4

A match must start within 5 minutes of its scheduled time. No additional time is granted after that window closes.

CHAPTER 06
Playoffs
⌄
1

Qualifier 1 — #1 vs #2. Winner advances straight to the Final; loser drops to Qualifier 2.

2

Eliminator 1 — #4 vs #5. Winner advances to Eliminator 2.

3

Eliminator 2 — Winner of Eliminator 1 vs #3. Winner advances to Qualifier 2.

4

Qualifier 2 — Winner of Eliminator 2 vs loser of Qualifier 1. Winner advances to the Final.

5

Final — Winner of Qualifier 1 vs winner of Qualifier 2. The winner is crowned FREEFIREXIPL Season 12 Champion.

CHAPTER 08
Fair Play & Conduct
⌄
1

Zero tolerance for hacks, macros, aimbots, or any third-party software that alters gameplay.

2

No teaming or exchanging kills with rival squads — every match is played to win.

3

Exploiting map or matchmaking bugs to gain an unfair advantage is prohibited.

4

A match must start within 5 minutes of the scheduled time — no exceptions once that window closes.

5

Harassment, hate speech or toxicity in voice or text chat is grounds for immediate review.

6

Organizers may require a screen-share or spectator check on any player, at any time, without prior notice.

🚨
Zero-Tolerance NoticeConfirmed cheating results in an instant season-long ban for the player and a points deduction for the whole squad.

CHAPTER 10
Disputes & Appeals
⌄
1

Disputes must be filed via the official Discord within 1 hour of the match ending.

2

The admin panel reviews recorded footage and match logs within 24 hours. Their decision is final and binding.

3

Franchises may appeal a ban once per season with supporting evidence, reviewed by a neutral panel.
