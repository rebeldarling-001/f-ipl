Stage 01 · Open to everyone
Play-In Registration Opens

Players submit real name, Free Fire IGN, UID, level, a profile screenshot, WhatsApp number and a ₹12 payment screenshot. Free Fire level must be 50 or above — anything lower can't be submitted.

🎮 Level 50+ only ₹12 entry fee 🟡 Status: Pending

Stage 02 · Admin checkpoint
Admin Verifies & Approves

The admin panel reviews every submission — profile screenshot, UID, level and payment proof — then marks it Approved or Rejected. Only approved players move forward.

Pending → Approved 🔍 Payment verified
⌄

Stage 03 · Public page
Registered Players Hub

Approved players appear automatically on the Registered Players page — name, IGN, UID, level and photo, visible to everyone. This same list feeds the Play-Ins.

⚡ Auto-updates Approved only

Stage 04 · 3 days on the grind
Play-Ins: 9 Matches, 40 Players

3 Battle Royale matches a day for 3 days. Room ID and password are posted for every match. 1 kill = 1 point, a Booyah adds a 3 point bonus — scored automatically.

👤 40 players 🗓️ 9 BR matches 🎯 Kill + Booyah scoring

Stage 05 · Your Play-In résumé
Total Play-In Score Locked In

Points across all 9 matches accumulate into one total — kills, Booyahs and match points per player. This total becomes each player's base value walking into the auction.

📌 Sets auction base price
⌄

Stage 06 · Live, animated auction room
Auction Day — Captains Bid Live

Only approved, Play-In players go under the hammer. Every viewer watches sold, unsold and going-once moments live — no refresh needed.

💰 ₹100 Cr purse ⬆️ Min bid ₹2 Cr, even only 👥 5 players per team 💸 Player gets 50% payout

Main Season — 14 Matches Each

All 8 fully-formed franchises battle it out. Every result updates the points table and player stats in real time.

Stage 09 · The race to Top 5
Points Table & Leaderboard

Teams are ranked by points, then round difference as the tiebreaker. Player stats — kills, Booyahs, total points — climb the leaderboard toward the Most Kills / MVP race.

📐 Sort: Points → Round Diff 🚀 Top 5 qualify

Playoffs — Top 5 Bracket

#1 vs #2 in Qualifier 1, #4 vs #5 in Eliminator 1 — the loser's path runs through Eliminator 2 and Qualifier 2 to earn a second shot at the Final.

🥊 Qualifier 1 & 2 ❌ Eliminator 1 & 2

Stage 11 · The last stop 🎉
Grand Final — Season 12 Champion

Winner of Qualifier 1 meets the winner of Qualifier 2. Whoever takes it lifts the FREEFIREXIPL Season 12 trophy, with the MVP crowned separately for most kills in the season.

🥇 Champion ₹600 🥈 Runner-up ₹300 👑 MVP ₹100
