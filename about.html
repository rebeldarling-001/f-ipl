About XIPL

The story behind the tournament, the people running it, and how to reach us.

🔥 SEASON 11 · FAN-RUN SINCE SEASON 1

FREEFIRE XIPL is a fan-made Free Fire franchise tournament — built by players, for players. What started as a scrappy weekend bracket among a few squads has grown into a full franchise league, complete with a player auction, a season-long points race and a Grand Finale that decides who gets etched into the Hall of Fame.

We're not affiliated with Garena — this is a passion project run by a small volunteer team who wanted a structured, fair, competitive space for the community to play the game they love at a higher level.

Every season, twelve franchises battle it out across group stage, playoffs and a Grand Finale, all governed by the same rulebook and scored the same way. No shortcuts, no favorites — just the arena deciding who's best.
